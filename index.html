Why do commercial Hearts games feel rigged?

Most commercial card apps use "rubber-banding" AI that peeks at your hidden hand and coordinates bots to dump the Queen of Spades on the human player to artificially manipulate win rates.

How Fair Hearts guarantees 100% fairness:

  • Zero Information Leakage: The AI functions receive only their own cards and publicly played cards. There is literally no code allowing them to peek at your hand.
  • Radical Symmetry: In the algorithm, all 4 seats are identical anonymous competitors. The bot does not even know which seat is human.
  • Independent Self-Interest: Every bot plays strictly to minimize its own score or win the match. When dumping penalty cards, bots target whoever is leading the scoreboard, never a specific player.
  • Open Source: The complete logic is readable in your browser console under window.Hearts.
